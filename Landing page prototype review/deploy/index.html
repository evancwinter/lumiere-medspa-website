Questions we hear every day

Not here. Our philosophy is conservative by default: we'd rather have you come back for a small touch-up than walk out overdone. Every plan is designed to keep your expressions yours. The goal is that people say you look rested, not that they ask what you had done.

Most treatments feel like a quick pinch — injectables take under 15 minutes, and we use topical numbing for anything more involved like microneedling. The most common thing we hear afterward is 'that was it?'

Exactly what's on this page. Botox from $12/unit, HydraFacial $199 — no consult-room surprise pricing. At your consultation you'll get a written quote before anything is scheduled, and payment plans are available through Cherry if you'd like to split it up.

Every treatment plan at Lumière is overseen by Dr. Camille Reyes, a board-certified physician with 10 years in aesthetic medicine — this is a medical practice, not a franchise with a rotating injector. We'll review your health history before recommending anything, and we'll decline treatments that aren't right for you.

Most of our clients book on a lunch break and go back to work. Injectables: no downtime, just no gym for 24 hours. Microneedling: a day or two of redness, like a mild sunburn. We'll tell you exactly what to expect for your specific treatment before you book it.

A conversation, not a pitch. You'll sit down with Dr. Reyes, talk through what's bothering you and what your goals are, and get honest recommendations built around your goals. Consultations are free, and you'll never be pressured to book treatment the same day.
