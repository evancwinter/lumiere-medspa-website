Will I look frozen?

Not here. Our approach is conservative by default. We would rather have you come back for a small touch-up than walk out overdone.

Every plan is built to keep your expressions yours. The goal is that people say you look rested, not that they ask what you had done.

Book a consultation

Does it hurt?

It feels like a quick pinch. Most people are surprised by how little there is to it. The most common thing we hear afterward is "that was it?"

How long does the visit take?

The treatment itself takes under fifteen minutes. Many of our clients book on a lunch break and go straight back to work.

What about downtime?

None. Skip the gym for the rest of the day, and that is the whole list. We will tell you exactly what to expect for your own plan before you book it.

When will I see the result?

Not the same day. The result settles in over a few weeks. The before-and-after photos on our site are taken a few weeks after treatment, which is when it has settled.

Who does the injecting?

Dr. Vivienne Ashby, a board-certified physician in aesthetic medicine. Every treatment plan at Lumière is hers, start to finish. There is no rotating injector.

She reviews your health history before recommending anything, and she declines treatments that are not right for you.

About Dr. Ashby

What does it cost?

Botox is priced per unit, and the price is listed on our services page before you book. At your consultation you get a written quote before anything is scheduled. There is no consult-room surprise.

See the services page

What happens at a first visit?

A conversation, not a pitch. You sit down with Dr. Ashby, talk through what is bothering you and what you want, and get honest recommendations built around that.

New clients are never treated on day one, and you will never be pressured to book treatment the same day.

What if it is not right for me?

Then we say so. Some skin, some histories and some goals are not the right fit for this treatment. If that is you, Dr. Ashby will tell you, and point you to the option that will actually work, or to someone who can help.

What happens afterward?

Every injectable appointment includes a complimentary follow-up two weeks later. If your result needs refining, we refine it.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or reschedule free up to a day ahead, right from your confirmation email. No phone call required.
